The journal uses **double-blind peer review**: reviewers do not know who the authors are, and authors do not know who the reviewers are. Please read the [author guidelines](/author-guidelines/) before you submit.

Fill in the details of all authors in the form. Upload the manuscript **without** author names, affiliations, acknowledgements or funding. The editor sees your details; reviewers receive only the anonymized file.

There are no submission or publication fees.
