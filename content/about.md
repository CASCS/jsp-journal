title: About
---
The Journal of Strength and Performance is an open‑access venue for experimental findings that advance human performance. We publish short, rigorous studies focused on strength, power, speed, conditioning, biomechanics, neuromuscular adaptations, and applied training science.

Our goal is to give visibility to the results that matter in real‑world performance—whether positive, negative, null, or exploratory. We welcome reproducibility studies, pilot data, applied experiments, and focused research addressing a single experimental question. Articles are accepted based on scientific rigor and methodological soundness, not on the size or direction of the results.
